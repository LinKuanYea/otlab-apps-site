怎麼玩

1. 拖方塊

把下方的方塊拖進 8×8 棋盤。方塊不能旋轉，三個都放完才會補新的三個。

2. 排滿就消除

任何一行或一列排滿就會消除，一次消越多條分數越高，連續消除還有連擊加成。所有方塊都放不下時，這局結束。

3. 橘貓

橘貓坐的那一格不能放方塊，但算作已經填滿，可以幫你湊滿一行。消除有貓的那一行或一列時，分數加倍，貓會被吵醒跳走。貓每隔幾手就會換位置，而且喜歡往方塊多的地方鑽。

4. 貓技能

每局開始前選一個技能帶上場。消除會集氣，集滿後橘貓會發光，點牠就能發動：

  • 貓掌橫掃：在棋盤上滑動選方向，清掉一整排或一整列。
  • 貓撲：拖曳瞄準任一格，放開後清掉以那格為中心的 3×3。

常見問題

怎麼集氣？

每消一條線集 1 點，消到有貓的線再多 2 點。技能本身清掉的格子不集氣。

沒有位置可以放了，一定結束嗎？

如果集氣已經滿了，遊戲不會馬上結束，橘貓會提醒你可以發動技能救命。不想用的話，也可以選擇直接結束這局。

發動技能後什麼都沒發生？

如果瞄準的範圍裡沒有方塊，技能不會發動，也不會扣掉集氣，貓會搖搖頭提醒你。換個位置再試一次就好。

瞄準到一半想取消？

把手指拖到棋盤外再放開，或點棋盤外的地方，就會取消。

我的進度和最高分會不見嗎？

最高分、設定和進行中的對局都只存在你的手機上。關掉 App 之後可以繼續上一局。刪除 App 會清除這些資料，也不會在不同裝置之間同步。

要付錢嗎？有廣告嗎？

完全免費，沒有廣告，也沒有內購。

支援哪些裝置？

需要 iOS 18 以上的 iPhone。

可以關掉震動嗎？

可以，在 App 的設定裡關閉觸覺回饋。系統設定裡的「減少動態效果」也會讓遊戲的動畫變得比較少。

meowBlock Support

A block puzzle game with an orange cat sitting on your board. Free, no ads, and no data collected.

Found a problem or have a suggestion? Email meowblock@otlab.org. When reporting a bug, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

How to play

1. Drag blocks

Drag pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Pieces can’t be rotated. You get three new pieces after placing all three.

2. Fill a line to clear it

Any full row or column clears. Clearing more lines at once scores more, and back-to-back clears earn a combo bonus. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

3. The orange cat

You can’t place a block on the cat’s square, but it counts as filled, so the cat can help you complete a line. Clear a line with the cat in it to double the points and wake the cat up. Every few moves the cat moves, and it likes to squeeze into crowded spots.

4. Cat skills

Pick one skill before each game. Clearing lines charges it; when it’s full the cat glows. Tap the cat to use it:

  • Paw Sweep: swipe on the board to choose a direction and clear a whole row or column.
  • Pounce: drag to aim at any square, then release to clear the 3×3 area around it.

FAQ

How do I charge my skill?

Each cleared line adds 1. A line with the cat in it adds 2 more. Squares cleared by a skill don’t add charge.

No room left — is the game over?

If your skill is fully charged, the game waits and the cat reminds you that you can use your skill to get unstuck. You can also choose to end the game instead.

I used my skill and nothing happened.

If there are no blocks in the target area, the skill doesn’t fire and you keep your charge. The cat shakes its head to let you know. Just aim somewhere else.

How do I cancel aiming?

Drag your finger off the board and release, or tap outside the board.

Will I lose my progress or high score?

Your high score, settings, and game in progress are stored only on your iPhone, so you can resume after closing the app. Deleting the app erases them, and they don’t sync between devices.

Does it cost anything? Are there ads?

It’s completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Can I turn off vibration?

Yes, turn off haptics in the app’s settings. The system “Reduce Motion” setting also reduces the game’s animations.
